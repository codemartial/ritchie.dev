The installer picks the archive for your machine, checks its SHA-256 checksum and puts ritchie, rcc and rcrun in ~/.local/bin. To install somewhere else, set RITCHIE_BIN_DIR:

Requirements

Ritchie compiles programs to Go and builds them with your Go toolchain, so Go 1.27 or newer must be on your PATH. The installer checks for Go and tells you if it is missing or too old.

Usage

ritchie compile compiles Ritchie source and ritchie run builds and runs a program. rcc and rcrun are short names for those two commands.
